Treat it as image-only/non-editable context. The runnable harness, replay recordings, generated scorecard, and generated charts are the source of truth for the benchmark.

Known wording caveats to preserve in any updated deck:

- Linear's roughly `14x` claim is the overall Linear connector result across search, get, and list, not a list-only claim.
- Native MCP outputs are mixed shapes across connectors, not always markdown or prose.
